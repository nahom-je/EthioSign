## 4.2 Verified Continuous Sentences (CESLR Benchmark)

EthSL grammar does not follow English or Amharic word-for-word word order. The continuous sentences evaluated in EthioSign include:

1. **`አባቴ ዘመድ ይወዳል`**  
   *Translation*: "My father loves his relatives."  
   *Grammar*: Subject [አባቴ] + Object [ዘመድ] + Verb [ይወዳል] (SOV word order).

2. **`እናቴ ዳቦ ትወዳለች`**  
   *Translation*: "My mother likes bread."  
   *Grammar*: Subject [እናቴ] + Object [ዳቦ] + Inflected Verb [ትወዳለች].

3. **`ሆስፒታሉ የት ነው`**  
   *Translation*: "Where is the hospital?"  
   *Grammar*: Topic [ሆስፒታሉ] + Interrogative [የት] + Copula [ነው] with obligatory furrowed eyebrow non-manual marker.

4. **`የኢትዮጵያ ባንዲራ ቀይ ቢጫ አረንጓዴ ነው`**  
   *Translation*: "The Ethiopian flag is red, yellow, and green."  
   *Grammar*: Genitive Civic [የኢትዮጵያ] + Subject [ባንዲራ] + Color Series [ቀይ ቢጫ አረንጓዴ] + Copula [ነው].
